# Jabvox

The modern customer support platform — omnichannel conversations, AI-assisted replies, help center, and reporting in a single self-hosted application.

> This is a private, proprietary repository. All rights reserved.

---

Jabvox centralizes all customer conversations into one powerful inbox, giving your team full control over customer data while providing the tools to deliver an exceptional support experience at scale.

### ✨ Captain – AI Agent for Support

Automate responses, handle common queries, and reduce agent workload so your team can focus on complex conversations while routine questions are resolved automatically.

### 💬 Omnichannel Support Desk

Bring every channel into one inbox: website live chat, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, Line, SMS and more.

### 📚 Help Center Portal

Publish help articles, FAQs, and guides so customers can find answers on their own.

### 🗂️ Other features

**Collaboration & Productivity** — Private notes & @mentions, labels, keyboard shortcuts & command bar, canned responses, auto-assignment, multilingual support, custom views & filters, business hours & auto-responders, teams & automation, agent capacity management.

**Customer Data & Segmentation** — Contact management, segments & notes, campaigns, custom attributes, pre-chat forms.

**Integrations** — Slack, Dialogflow, dashboard apps, Shopify, Google Translate, Linear.

**Reports & Insights** — Live view, conversation/agent/inbox/label/team reports, CSAT reports, downloadable reports.
